# Nukalhed22 Studios — Project Goal

The goal of Nukalhed22 Studios is to develop an interactive guitar-learning application that transforms any supported song into a practical, visual guide for playing guitar.

Users will be able to upload an audio file or find a song through an authorized YouTube integration. The application will analyze the song to identify its musical key, tempo, chord progression, and chord changes, then translate that information into clear, actionable guitar-playing instructions.

The core objective is to show guitarists exactly where to play on the guitar neck, which scale to use, what fret to start on, and how to move between connected scale positions to play lead guitar over a song.

The application will feature an interactive fretboard that displays scale shapes, root notes, chord tones, and recommended target notes in synchronization with the music. Users will be able to switch between low, middle, and high fretboard positions, explore alternative scales, and learn how different notes relate to the current chord.

The long-term vision is to help guitarists move beyond memorizing isolated pentatonic boxes and develop a deeper understanding of the entire fretboard, musical phrasing, and improvisation.

The application should make music theory practical, accessible, and immediately useful, allowing guitarists of different skill levels to understand what they are playing, why it works, and where to go next.

The ultimate goal is to turn any supported song into an interactive guitar lesson that connects music analysis, chord progressions, scale selection, and fretboard navigation in one seamless experience.
